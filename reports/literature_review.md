# Literature review: region-resolved brain-MRI evidence audit

Search date: 2026-10-29 (Asia/Kolkata). Sources searched: publisher pages or open full text where available, PubMed/PMC, arXiv, OpenAlex, and web search. Search strings: `"Clever Hans effect found in a widely used brain tumour MRI dataset"`; `"Mask of Truth: Model Sensitivity to Unexpected Regions of Medical Images"`; `"BRISC" "s41597-026-06753-y"`; `"s41598-021-01681-w" brain MRI data leakage`; `"TACE" "brain MRI" counterfactual ROI methods`; `"brain tumor" "matched sham" masking counterfactual MRI`; and `"brain tumor MRI" "peritumoral" "counterfactual" classification`.

Inclusion criteria were works that report methods relevant to MRI tumour classification, shortcut/region masking, counterfactual image editing, peritumoural context, leakage, or the two local datasets. Excluded from the closest-method comparison were generic saliency-only papers and papers without a describable intervention or data-integrity method. This is a documented, not systematic-review, search; no claim of exhaustive retrieval is made.

## Implications for protocol

The accessible literature already prevents any “first-ever” claim for tumour-region/context analysis or tumour-aware counterfactual explanation. It supports a narrower question: how do *fixed, annotated-mask-defined* core, immediate-margin, outer-margin and remote non-lesion edits change a trained classifier’s true-class score relative to same-method matched shams, and does that sensitivity vary by architecture, edit method, dataset or plane? The evidence below does not establish biological causation, clinical reasoning, or realism of an edited MRI.

## Sources examined

- Cheng et al. full-text methods: 2015 *PLOS ONE* article, including the 233-patient grouping, image counts, manual border annotation, dilation/ring partition and five-fold patient CV.
- TACE arXiv record and method summary: submitted 2024-09-19; accepted at WIRN 2024 according to its arXiv record.
- Sourget et al. publisher/PMC record: ROI-masking study on chest radiographs and fundus images, not brain MRI.
- Wallis & Buvat publisher record: shortcut/bias finding in the commonly used brain-tumour dataset.
- BRISC data descriptor/release documentation and local README/manifest schema.
- Yagis et al. open Scientific Reports record/PDF search result: subject versus slice-level split analysis.

No retrieved source was treated as proof that an unsearched design does not exist.
