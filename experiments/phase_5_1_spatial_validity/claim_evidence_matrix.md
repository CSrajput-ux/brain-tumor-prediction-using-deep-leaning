## Evaluation Framework and Evidence Status Legend

Each claim from the Phase 5 investigation is evaluated against strict causal inference criteria, geometric invariants, and confounder analyses. Statuses are strictly restricted to:
- **`SUPPORTED_WITHIN_TESTED_SETUP`**: Directly verified by reproduced point estimates, paired bootstrap CIs, and matched controls within the tested dataset and frozen model architecture.
- **`PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`**: Empirical effect is observed, but effect size falls short of pre-registered thresholds or contains known confounds.
- **`UNRESOLVED`**: Observed effect is severely confounded with competing mechanisms (e.g. radiometric shifts, boundary artifacts) that cannot be disentangled without further controls.
- **`NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`**: The empirical evidence contradicts the claim.
- **`NOT_ASSESSED`**: Not directly tested in the current experimental scope.
