# Shortlist of Target Springer Journals for Manuscript Submission

**Scope of Study:** Controlled evaluation of shortcut learning, spatial-context sensitivity, and synthetic boundary artifacts in deep CNN brain MRI classification on the Figshare 1512427 cohort.

---

## 1. Primary Recommendation: *Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine* (JIIM, formerly *Journal of Digital Imaging*)

- **Publisher:** Springer Nature (Official Journal of the Society for Imaging Informatics in Medicine - SIIM)
- **Aims & Scope:** Clinical medical imaging informatics, algorithmic auditing, benchmark validity, dataset leakage, AI evaluation methodologies, PACS/DICOM integrity.
- **Why It Fits Perfectly:**
  - The study's central contribution is a methodological and empirical audit of public brain MRI benchmark validity, demonstrating that reported high accuracy can mask complete vulnerability to non-anatomical spatial interventions.
  - JIIM specifically prioritizes research addressing dataset contamination, split integrity, and the translation gap of radiological AI.
- **Submission Parameters:**
  - **Article Type:** Original Research Paper
  - **Abstract Limit:** 250 words (Structured: Purpose, Methods, Results, Conclusion)
  - **Word Limit:** Typically 4,000–5,500 words (excluding references, tables, captions)
  - **Review Model:** Single-blind peer review
  - **Reference Format:** Springer Vancouver / Numbered citations
  - **Data/Code Sharing:** Mandates Data Availability and Code Availability statements.

---

## 2. Strong Second Option: *Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing* (MBEC)

- **Publisher:** Springer Nature (Official Journal of the International Federation for Medical and Biological Engineering - IFMBE)
- **Aims & Scope:** Advanced computational modeling, medical image processing, neural network interpretability, biomedical signal analysis.
- **Why It Fits:**
  - Rigorous engineering focus on image processing controls (seam-suppressed blending, Navier-Stokes inpainting, Fourier spectral analysis, paired cluster bootstrap).
- **Submission Parameters:**
  - **Article Type:** Original Scientific Paper
  - **Abstract Limit:** 200–250 words
  - **Word Limit:** Up to 6,000 words
  - **Review Model:** Single-blind peer review
  - **Reference Format:** Springer basic numbered or name-year style.

---

## 3. Specialized Neuroimaging Option: *Neuroinformatics*

- **Publisher:** Springer Nature
- **Aims & Scope:** Computational methods, data management, algorithmic validation, and tool dissemination in structural and functional neuroimaging.
- **Why It Fits:**
  - Focuses specifically on neurological data governance, brain MRI reproducibility, and open-source validation pipelines.
- **Review Model:** Single-blind peer review.

---

## 4. Editorial Decision Recommendation

- **Target:** **Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine (JIIM)**.
- **Contingency / Scope Pivot:** If the manuscript emphasizes mathematical boundary filtering and Fourier artifact physics, **MBEC** is the optimal alternate venue.
